Having completed the internship program at **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** (April 17, 2026 – July 10, 2026), I would like to dedicate this section to sharing my honest impressions and constructive suggestions to help the program continue to grow and improve.

### General Feedback

**1. Learning Environment & Community**

The FCAJ environment is remarkably open and welcoming. The most impressive aspect is the absence of barriers — whether you are a first-year student or a working professional, everyone is equally welcomed, encouraged to share, and ready to support one another. The Community Days and Meetups are the clearest proof of this: an atmosphere that is both professional and approachable, genuinely motivating participants to continue their Cloud learning journey.

**2. Support from the Admin Team & Mentor**

The FCAJ admin team is highly responsive and dedicated throughout the internship. Notably, **Nguyen Hoang Long** and **Thinh Nguyen** directly guided interns through the internship process, answered technical questions, and ensured that everyone had full access to community events and resources.

The program also benefits from the accompaniment of mentor **Nguyen Gia Hung**. The speakers at the meetups are all seasoned practitioners — they share not only technical knowledge but also real-world work mindsets and career direction.

**3. Relevance to My Academic Background**

The program's content (AWS Cloud, Serverless, AI/ML, DevOps) aligns well with career development goals in the IT industry. The **AutoRx** internship project was highly practical — it required me to actually design, implement, and debug a real end-to-end Cloud system, far beyond what can be achieved in a classroom setting.

**4. Learning Opportunities & Skill Development**

The program offered multi-dimensional growth: technical skills (AWS services, Prompt Engineering, BMAD Method) alongside soft skills (presentation, technical writing, Builder mindset). This combination is something very few traditional internship programs offer.

**5. Community Culture & Team Spirit**

FCAJ has a strongly positive sharing culture — everyone is encouraged to stand up, ask questions, and even become a speaker themselves. This is entirely different from the "fear of being wrong or embarrassed" attitude common in academic environments, and it genuinely helped me become more confident in expressing and presenting myself.

**6. Policies & Benefits for Interns**

The program offers flexible scheduling, allowing interns to manage their cloud learning alongside university coursework. Being able to attend real-world Community Days and Meetups is a genuinely valuable benefit that most traditional internship programs do not provide.

---

### What I Was Most Satisfied With

What I valued most was the opportunity to **genuinely build a real Cloud system from scratch** — from designing the architecture, writing Lambda backend logic, connecting API Gateway, all the way to deploying and monitoring on AWS. This is hands-on experience that is very difficult to replicate in a university course.

Additionally, being exposed to the Cloud professional community and listening to real-world stories from speakers like **Nguyen Duy Khang** (Solutions Architect) and **Nguyen Tuan Thinh** (DevOps/Cloud Engineer) gave me a much clearer picture of the career path I want to pursue.

---

### What the Program Could Improve

- **Add more structured hands-on lab sessions:** The meetups are highly valuable but are predominantly listening-based. Adding interactive hands-on sessions directly on the AWS Console would help newcomers absorb concepts much faster.
- **Provide a clearer roadmap for each phase:** A structured weekly/monthly learning path with explicit milestones would help interns track their progress more effectively.
- **Strengthen peer-to-peer connections:** Creating smaller specialty-focused groups (DevOps, AI/ML, Backend...) or networking sessions would allow members to support each other in deeper, more focused learning.

---

### Would I Recommend This Program to Friends?

**Yes, absolutely.** FCAJ is not just a technical internship program — it is a genuine learning community. If you want to enter the Cloud and AI field and are looking for an environment where you can both learn and build meaningful connections, this is a highly worthwhile investment of your time.

---

### Suggestions & Wishes

- I hope the program continues to host Community Days on a regular monthly schedule — these events are the single biggest contributor to FCAJ's unique community value.
- I hope there will be more opportunities for interns to present their final projects to the broader community and receive feedback from diverse perspectives.
- I hope FCAJ continues to expand its speaker network across more specialized domains (Game Cloud, FinTech, EdTech...) to bring greater diversity in how Cloud technology is applied in real-world industries.
