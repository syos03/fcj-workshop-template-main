During my internship, I participated in two community events organized by the First Cloud Journey (FCAJ) program. Each event was a memorable learning experience combining technical knowledge, soft skills, and valuable networking opportunities.

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** FCAJ Community Day — 09/05/2026

&emsp;**Date & Time:** 09/05/2026

&emsp;**Location:** Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee

&emsp;**Summary:** A community meetup featuring 4 speakers covering learning psychology & dopamine-driven habits (Huynh Hoang Long), Automated Prompt Engineering & Proptimizer on AWS Serverless (Nguyen Tuan Thinh), the role of AI and foundational knowledge for fresher developers (Nguyen Duy Khang), and structured AI-driven development using the BMAD Method (Thao Nguyen).
